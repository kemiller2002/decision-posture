About

Clarity exists to make organizational belief visible enough to manage.

The framework is built around a simple observation: groups often keep acting on stale beliefs long after reality has shifted.

What it is

A serious working framework for making claims, assumptions, evidence, confidence, ownership, and reassessment explicit.

What it is not

It is not motivational language, a general-purpose productivity method, or a promise that better terminology alone solves coordination problems.

How to use it

Use it to examine real work: projects, reviews, audits, postmortems, strategy claims, and operating assumptions.

Decision Posture remains part of the language, but the broader aim is adaptation: helping teams notice when the beliefs beneath action have stopped matching reality.
