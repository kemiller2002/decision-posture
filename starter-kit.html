Starter Kit

The Clarity Quick Assessment is a lightweight way to examine the beliefs behind a project, decision, proposal, architecture, methodology, or strategy. It is designed to be usable in 15–30 minutes.

The site explains Clarity. The Starter Kit lets someone try it.

Clarity is a framework for making beliefs, assumptions, evidence, confidence, ownership, and reassessment explicit so people can adapt when reality changes.

Clarity Quick Assessment v1

Claim

What are we acting as if true?

Posture

Discovery / Commitment / Reassessment

Evidence

What evidence supports this claim?

Assumptions

What must be true for this claim to hold?

Confidence

Low / Moderate / High

Owner

Who owns this claim and its consequences?

Reassessment Trigger

What would change our minds?

Recommended Action

Proceed / gather evidence / reduce scope / reassess / adapt

How To Use It

1. Pick one claim.

2. Assign a posture.

3. List the evidence.

4. Surface assumptions.

5. Check whether confidence exceeds evidence.

6. Define one reassessment trigger.

7. Decide the next action.

Good First Uses

A project that feels stuck

A proposal that sounds convincing but untested

A product idea

An architecture decision

A methodology change

A postmortem explanation

A strategy that depends on assumptions
